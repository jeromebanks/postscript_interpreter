Reference and interpretation

Mochi in Denim Blue begins with a real photograph rather than a geometric system. The interpretation keeps Mochi's upright ears, orange-sable and cream markings, enormous reflected eyes, compact dark nose, and denim-blue setting. It trades individual hairs and clothing folds for directional loaded-brush masses and cross-woven canvas marks; the aim is a recognizable oil study, not a traced copy or a claim of physical pigment simulation.

Style packs

Four motif libraries in lib/styles/, layered on the generative-art toolkit (lib/artkit.ps — seeded random, mood palettes, turtle graphics, an L-system engine, and the pathforall-powered alongpath/ pathtext brushes). Each pack adds three palettes and a drawer of motifs; the psart skill (.claude/skills/psart/SKILL.md) teaches the whole render-look-refine workflow to any agent. Specimen posters below are examples/style_*.ps, 620×800, deterministic under srand.

The font library

Original Type 3 display faces (lib/fonts/) — every glyph is a program, so the letterforms do things outlines can't. Self-contained files; each runs unchanged in Ghostscript.

The specimen poster/Neon (glow by overdraw), /Marquee (bulbs, glints, burnouts), /Constellation (star charts), /Lapidary (chiseled stone)
The second folio/Circuitry (copper traces, pads, and vias), /Stitchwork (cross-stitch on the 45° grid), /Confetti (a thrown handful, still falling)
/HandScriptDynamic handwriting — rand-jittered strokes, wandering baseline, varying pen pressure; no two letters ever match

The font catalog

Beyond the Type 3 library, fonts/catalog/ holds 58 libre outline faces loaded at findfont time — the complete LaserWriter 35 resolved to metric-compatible faces (TeX Gyre, URW symbols) plus a display shelf from garalde to arcade. pscat --fonts lists every name. The Fonts page shows every face individually, Type 3 library included.
